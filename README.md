# Meowl Ft. Jace

Auto delivery for Steal a Brainrot sales on Discord, through the JACE middleman.

The app posts your stock, answers buyers in DMs, follows the JACE Auto Middleman ticket, checks the Litecoin payment on chain and delivers the pets in game.

## Download

**[Download MeowlFtJaceSetup.exe](https://github.com/marselow/meowl-jace/releases/latest/download/MeowlFtJaceSetup.exe)**

The installer is small and always installs the latest version: it downloads the app (about 80 MB) while it installs. After that, the app updates itself. Older versions stop working once a newer one is required, so there is no reason to keep an old copy.

Release notes are in [Releases](https://github.com/marselow/meowl-jace/releases).

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- A JACE account. Access is invite-only: you sign in with Discord after you get access in [discord.gg/meowl-lister](https://discord.gg/meowl-lister).
- Your Luarmor key. The scanner and the delivery script come through Luarmor, never from the app.

## Install

1. Run `MeowlFtJaceSetup.exe` with the internet on. It downloads the latest version of the app.
2. If Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The installer is not code-signed yet.
3. Open **Meowl Ft. Jace** and sign in with Discord.

## For buyers

How buying through the JACE middleman works: [meowl-lister.online/jacemm](https://meowl-lister.online/jacemm/)

## Good to know

- The app uses your own Discord account to answer DMs and to act in JACE tickets. Automating a user account goes against Discord's Terms of Service and can get the account banned. Use it at your own risk.
- This repository only hosts the installer. The source code is not public.
